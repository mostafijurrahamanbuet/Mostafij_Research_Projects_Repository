
# Analytical and Simulation Stress Analysis of Bicycle Components

**Undergraduate Engineering Project | Machine Design | Mechanical Engineering, BUET**

**Project Areas:** Engineering Mechanics | Stress Analysis | Mechanical Design | Structural Mechanics

---

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the analytical evaluation of stresses in bicycle components using fundamental principles of engineering mechanics and machine design and then verify it using simulation.

The study examines mechanical loading and the resulting stress distributions to understand the structural behavior of selected bicycle components.

The project applies both analytical and simulation engineering methods to evaluate component loading, stresses, and relevant mechanical design considerations.

---

## Engineering Methods

- Engineering Mechanics
- Analytical Stress Calculations
- Mechanical Loading Analysis
- Strength of Materials
- Machine Design Principles
