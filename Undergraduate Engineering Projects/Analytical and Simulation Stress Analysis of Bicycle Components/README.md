  

# Analytical and Simulation Stress Analysis of Bicycle Components

**Undergraduate Engineering Project | Machine Design | Mechanical Engineering, BUET**

**Project Areas:** Engineering Mechanics | Stress Analysis | Mechanical Design | Structural Mechanics | Engineering Simulation

---

## Project Overview

This project investigates the structural behavior of bicycle components through analytical stress calculations and engineering simulation.

The work involves evaluating mechanical loading and the resulting stresses using fundamental principles of engineering mechanics, strength of materials, and machine design.

Analytical calculations were performed to determine the stresses developed in selected bicycle components. The analytical results were subsequently examined using engineering simulation to assess the structural response.

The project demonstrates the application of analytical and computational engineering methods to mechanical component analysis.
