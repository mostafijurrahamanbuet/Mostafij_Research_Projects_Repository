
# Design and Fabrication of a Solar Collector Thermosyphon-Type Heat Exchanger

**Undergraduate Engineering Project | Heat Transfer | Mechanical Engineering, BUET**

**Project Areas:** Thermal System Design | Heat Exchangers | Solar Thermal Energy | HTRI | Fabrication

---

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the design and fabrication of a solar-collector thermosyphon-type double-pipe heat exchanger incorporating longitudinal fins.

The work involved applying fundamental heat transfer principles and thermal engineering design methods to develop the heat exchanger configuration.

**HTRI (Heat Transfer Research, Inc.) software** was used as part of the engineering design process to support heat exchanger analysis and design considerations.

The project provided practical experience in thermal system design, heat transfer engineering, and mechanical fabrication.

## Design and Methodology

The project involved:

1. **Thermal System Design:** Developing the heat exchanger configuration based on heat transfer principles.
2. **Heat Exchanger Geometry:** Incorporating a double-pipe arrangement with longitudinal fins.
3. **HTRI-Based Analysis:** Using HTRI software to support thermal design and engineering calculations.
4. **Fabrication:** Constructing the thermosyphon-type heat exchanger based on the developed design.
