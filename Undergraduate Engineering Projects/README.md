
# Undergraduate Engineering Projects

This directory contains selected undergraduate engineering projects completed during my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).

The projects cover mathematical modeling, control engineering, thermal system design, and mechanical stress analysis.

## Projects

1. **Two-Wheeled Self-Balancing Robot**  
   Dynamic modeling, PID control, and metaheuristic optimization.

2. **Solar Thermosyphon Heat Exchanger**  
   Thermal system design, heat transfer analysis, and fabrication.

3. **Analytical Stress Analysis of Bicycle Components**  
   Mechanical loading, stress calculations, and machine design.

Each project will be documented through its technical description and available project report.
