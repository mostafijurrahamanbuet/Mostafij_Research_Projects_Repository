## Presentation Overview

This academic presentation introduces electric double-layer capacitors (EDLCs), highlighting their operating principles, importance in energy storage, and key engineering considerations.
It focuses on the simulation methodology of crumpled graphene-based EDLCs and the validation of previously published results through simulation.
